Free Universal Archive Extractor

Extract files from ZIP, RAR, 7-Zip, TAR, GZIP, and more, entirely in your browser. Everything happens locally — nothing is uploaded.

Drop an archive here, or click to choose a file
Supports .zip, .rar, .7z, .tar, .tar.gz, .tar.bz2 and more
    Powered by libarchive (via WebAssembly), the same engine behind the 7-Zip and unrar command-line tools.

    How this works: this page loads libarchive, compiled to WebAssembly, and runs it in a background worker in your browser tab. Your archive file is read locally and is never sent to any server.
